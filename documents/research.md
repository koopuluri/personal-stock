# Research

This file records potential areas of research and design exploration that are not part of the current personal-stock agreement.

### Optional collateral for payment obligations

The agreement gives shareholders contractual payment rights but no lien or security interest in the owner's assets. If a distribution or other payment becomes due and the owner fails to pay, shareholders may establish the amount through the agreement's records, audits, and dispute process, but they would still need to pursue recovery without dedicated collateral; this creates a narrow risk that shareholders could prove what they are owed yet be unable to collect it because assets were transferred, spent, reached first by other creditors, or involved in insolvency proceedings.

A potential future model would preserve the owner's legal ownership and control while allowing an independent collateral agent to hold a security interest, for shareholders' benefit, only in assets that can legally and practically be pledged. After an uncured payment default, the agent could enforce against enough pledged collateral to satisfy obligations already due, with any surplus remaining with the owner. Research would need to address compensation assets that prohibit pledging, perfection and priority requirements, bankruptcy treatment, enforcement triggers, tax consequences, administrative cost, and whether this limited protection justifies the added complexity.
